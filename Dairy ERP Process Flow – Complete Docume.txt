Dairy ERP Process Flow – Complete Documentation
1. Milk Arrival (Supplier → Plant Gate)
• Supplier brings milk in cans/tankers.
• Gate operator records:
  - Supplier/Route name
  - Vehicle number
  - Time of arrival
-add a field for adding challan/recipt
• Supervisor verifies details.
• ERP captures: Supplier ID, Route ID, Vehicle ID, Arrival Time, Approx Quantity.
2. Primary Quality Testing (Platform Tests)
Tests done:
  - FAT
  - SNF
  - Acidity %
  - Temperature
  - Alcohol/Adulteration test
  - COB test
  - Organoleptic analysis
Pass/Fail determines acceptance.
ERP captures: FAT, SNF, LR, Temperature, Water %, Adulteration result, Pass/Reject.
3. Milk Unloading & Weighing


• Unloaded into dump tank/silos.
• ERP records exact weight, supplier ID, quality score.
• Finance auto-calculates payment based on FAT-SNF.
4. Milk Chilling & Storage
• Milk chilled to 4°C.
• Stored in milk silos with agitators.
• ERP logs:
  - Silo number
  - Inlet time
  - Temperature logs
  - Volume
-AGitation run time boolean (is running or not)
• Maintenance monitors chilling plant & compressor.
5. Cream Separation & Standardization
• Milk sent to cream separator.
• FAT adjusted to create:
  - Toned
  - Standardized
  - Full cream
  - Skimmed milk
• ERP logs:
  - FAT before/after
  - Machine runtime
  - Standardization formula
  - Loss calculation
6. Pasteurization & Homogenization
Pasteurization: 72°C for 15 sec → 4°C cooling.
Homogenization: Fat globules evenly dispersed.(optional)
ERP logs:
  - Temperature chart
  - Holding time
  - Pressure logs(optional)
  - Batch number
Auto alerts for deviation.
7. Product Manufacturing (Curd, Ghee, Paneer, etc.)
Curd Process:
  1. Heat treatment
  2. Culture inoculation
  3. Incubation
  4. Breaking curd
  5. Filling

Paneer Process:
  1. Coagulation
  2. Pressing
  3. Cutting
  4. Packaging

Butter/Ghee:
  • Cream → Butter → Ghee heating

ERP logs: Batch, process parameters, yields.
8. Packaging
• Milk pouch filling
• Curd cup filling
• Bottle filling
ERP tracks:
  - Line number
  - Output
  - Rejected packs
  - Material usage
Maintenance tracks machine health.
9. Storage & Dispatch
• Products stored in cold room.
• Sales creates dispatch plan (route-wise).
• ERP tracks:
  - Batch-wise stock
  - FIFO
  - Delivery orders
  - Distributor mapping.
10. Finance Integration
Supplier side:
  - Milk payment (FAT-SNF)
  - Incentives(optional)

Sales side:
  - Dealer payment
  - Invoice generation
ERP provides full financial visibility.
11. HR Integration
• Shift allocation
• Attendance
• Duty logs
• Performance reports
• Training compliance.
12. R&D Integration
• New product trials
• Culture-temperature experiments
• Cost optimization
• Shelf-life testing
13. Management Dashboard
• Milk collection trends
• Quality trends
• Production output
• Sales & revenue
• Machine downtime
• HR performance
• Inventory status
• Profitability
